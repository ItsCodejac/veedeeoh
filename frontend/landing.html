About veedeeoh

veedeeoh is a web application for finding and watching films and television that are already free to watch. It does not host or licence any video. When you press play, the stream comes from the provider that holds it — Pluto TV, Tubi or the Internet Archive — directly to your browser, advertising included, exactly as those services deliver it.

What veedeeoh adds is everything around the video: one search across all three catalogues, up to three household profiles with their own watchlists and parental rating limits, resume-where-you-left-off across devices, a kids mode restricted to age-rated titles, and watch parties that keep a group in sync on the same film.

Accounts exist so those profiles, watchlists and resume points follow you between devices. Signing in with Google gives veedeeoh your name and email address for that purpose and nothing else. It is never used for advertising, never sold, and can be exported or deleted from Settings at any time. See our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

WATCH TOGETHER

Same film. Same second. No chat box.

Start a party, send one link, and everyone lands in the same film at the same frame. You control playback: when you pause, everyone pauses. When you skip back because someone missed a line, everyone skips back.

It stays in sync

Viewers reconcile against the host continuously, so a slow connection catches up instead of drifting a minute behind. If someone falls out of step there is one button to pull them back.

You decide who gets in

Set a seat limit and approve people at the door, or leave it open. Removing someone asks why: connection trouble and making room let them return, the other two do not.

Reactions, not a chat box

Six reactions float over the film and vanish. There is no free text, on purpose: a reaction cannot be used to say something targeted at somebody, and nothing anyone sends is kept.

Kids profiles stay protected

A rating limit is not suspended because the film is somebody else's pick. A kids profile is refused a party playing something outside its limits, rather than quietly let in.

Your friends do not need a card to join you

A free account can join four watch parties a month. No subscription, no trial countdown, no payment details. It exists so an invitation is something a person can simply accept.

Why we can give away half

Because the subscription is nearly all margin, and we would rather spend it on you than on advertising. The catalogue is free to carry, and one more person browsing costs us almost nothing to serve.

The one thing that does cost us real money is hosting watch parties, which runs on metered infrastructure we are billed for by the minute. So watch party credits are the part we keep, and they are not shared. Everything else splits down the middle.

Half of net revenue, meaning after tax, which is collected for a tax authority and was never ours to divide. Paid for the life of the referred account. Watch party credit top-ups are excluded.
