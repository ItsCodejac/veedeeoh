Kids curation

    

      TV-rated content gates itself, so it is not here. What is left are the G, PG and unrated
      films — the ones whose rating cannot be trusted, because PG-13 did not exist until 1984
      and pre-1984 PG absorbed what would now be PG-13. The War of the Worlds (1953) is rated G.
      

        Little — the TV-Y bar, which the
          guidelines define as ages 2–6, not expected to frighten younger children.
          That age range is the rating's own wording.
        Older — the TV-Y7 bar:
          age 7 and above, may include mild fantasy or comedic violence, may frighten children
          under 7. The guideline sets no upper age. This tier also admits TV-G, which
          means suitable for all ages and explicitly not designed specifically for
          children — so general-audience material belongs here too, not only kids' shows.
        Not sure — park it. Nothing is approved, so a child never sees it, and you
          can come back to the parked list whenever you like.
      

      
Watch anything you are unsure about. Judging by title is how a
      denylist once certified racist wartime cartoons as TV-Y7.
